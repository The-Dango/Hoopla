How to play

Placing things

Tap a square to cycle it. An X is your note that a hoop can't go there; it does nothing to the puzzle but keep you straight.

Drag across empty squares to X out a run of them at once. On a mouse, right-click puts a hoop down directly.

Every colour holds the same number

Usually one hoop, sometimes two. The chip at the top of the board tells you which.

The edge numbers count that row and column

Each one says how many hoops belong in its line. They show on all four sides, so you can read them from wherever you are looking.

Hoops never touch

Not side by side, not corner to corner. This is the rule that does most of the work.

When you're stuck

Check
Free. Says whether everything so far is right, never what's wrong.
Hint
Finds the simplest next step and explains why. Adds time to your score.
Menu
Save the board to come back to, or give up and see how it fits together.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and it never needs a guess.
